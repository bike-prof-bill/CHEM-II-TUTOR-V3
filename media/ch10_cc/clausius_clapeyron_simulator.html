[1] Clausius-Clapeyron Relation

Derived under assumptions of ideal vapor behavior and negligible condensed molar volume (ΔV ≈ Vg):

ln(P₂ / P₁) = -(ΔHvap / R) · (1/T₂ - 1/T₁)

Assuming a constant mean ΔHvap = 42.10 kJ/mol, the slope yields -5063.5 K relative to normal boiling at 82.3 °C.

[2] Antoine Equation (NIST Empirical)

Three-parameter empirical formulation accounting for temperature-dependent ΔHvap(T):

log₁₀(P / mmHg) = 8.00308 - [1505.52 / (T(°C) + 211.60)]

Parameters calibrated for 2-propanol across 0°C to 100°C. Non-idealities and hydrogen-bonding variations emerge at lower temperatures.
